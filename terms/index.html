What Quarter sheet does

Quarter sheet helps UK sole traders keep local income and expense records, group them into tax-year quarters and export those records. Version 1 does not connect to or submit updates to HMRC. It is not HMRC-recognised submission software.

Your responsibility

You are responsible for the accuracy, completeness and safekeeping of your records, choosing suitable categories, checking current filing dates, and submitting through HMRC-recognised software where required. Category references are organisational aids, not tax advice. Ask HMRC or a qualified tax professional when unsure.

Availability and backups

The free ledger is provided as available. Browser storage can be cleared by you, the browser or the device. Download encrypted backups and test your exported files before a deadline. We are not liable for loss caused by failing to keep an external backup.
