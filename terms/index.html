What Quarter sheet does

Quarter sheet helps UK sole traders keep income and expense records, group them into tax-year quarters, and export those records. It does not submit updates to HMRC or provide tax advice.

Your responsibility

You are responsible for your records, categories, filing dates, and choosing suitable accounting software. Category references are organisational aids. Ask HMRC or a qualified tax professional when unsure.

Availability and backups

The free ledger is provided as available. Browser storage can be cleared by you, the browser, or the device. Download encrypted backups and test exported files before a deadline.

Supporter access

Supporter access costs £19 once for a supporter badge and backup reminders. Sociobot/Dodo is merchant of record and handles payment and refunds. Core record keeping, receipts, backups, and exports remain free.

Acceptable use

Do not attempt to disrupt the service, bypass verification, or use it unlawfully. You may stop using the app and delete its site data at any time.

Changes

Tax rules and this product may change. Material term changes will be dated on this page. Existing records remain exportable through the free tools.
