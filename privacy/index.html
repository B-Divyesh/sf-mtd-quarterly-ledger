Privacy notice

Effective 28 August 2026

Your records stay in your browser

Quarter sheet stores transactions, notes and receipt files in your browser. We do not receive, view or sync this ledger data. Clearing site data can erase it, so make encrypted backups regularly.

Encrypted backups

Backup files are encrypted in your browser using AES-GCM with a key derived from your passphrase. They download directly to your device. We do not receive the file or passphrase and cannot recover either.

Supporter access

If you buy or restore supporter access, checkout and verification are handled by Sociobot’s billing API, with Dodo as merchant of record. The license token and a time-stamped verification result are stored in your browser. Your ledger is never sent with a verification request.

Network requests

The app uses no analytics, advertising, third-party fonts or tracking scripts. The ledger works offline after the first visit. A network request is made only when verifying supporter access or fetching application updates.

Generated artwork

The drafting-desk illustration was generated for Quarter sheet using the factory’s image model. It contains no real person or customer data.

Your choices

Use “Back up or restore records” to take a portable encrypted copy. Use your browser’s site-data controls to delete local records. Because we do not hold ledger data, there is no server-side account to access or delete.

Contact

For privacy questions, email privacy@sociobot.in.
